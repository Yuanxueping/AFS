Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: June 1, 2025

Introduction

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information this website collects, how it is used, and what choices you have regarding your data. By using this site, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to register or create an account to access content on this site. However, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Usage data — Pages visited, time spent on pages, referring URLs, browser type, and general geographic location (country/region level).
  • Device information — Browser version, operating system, and screen resolution, collected automatically when you visit the site.
  • Cookies — Small text files stored in your browser to improve your experience and enable advertising functionality (see below).

Cookies and Advertising

This website uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting aboutads.info.

How We Use Information

Information collected is used to:

  • Serve relevant advertising through our advertising partners.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect data independently:

  • Google AdSense — Advertising delivery and measurement.

These services operate under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our servers beyond what is necessary for site functionality. Analytics data retained by third-party providers is subject to their respective retention policies.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us via our Contact page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of this site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
